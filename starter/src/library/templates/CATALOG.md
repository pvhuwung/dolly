# Templates

GENERATED by `npm run library`: don't edit it. Whole scenes that take words as props. Put one in SCENES: `{name, seconds, component: () => <Statement text="…" />}`. They draw their own backdrop and lay themselves out for every format.

`preview.jpg` shows every one: each cell is an item's sample, labelled with its code.

## Statement · `templates/statement`

One big line filling the frame, with an optional eyebrow: a hook, a punchline or a turn in the story.

`<Statement eyebrow="Agents everywhere" text="Five agents running." accent="One needs you." />`

- `eyebrow?: string` A small label above the headline.
- `text: string` The line, before its accent phrase.
- `accent?: string` The phrase set in the style's accent way, after the text.
- `effect?: TextEffect` How the words come in; the style's own if not set.
- `exit?: number` Frame it starts to leave; it stays to the end if not set.

Needs: `text/kinetic`

## Metric · `templates/metric`

A number that counts up with what it counts under it; real numbers only.

`<Metric value={12} label="agents in one window" note="and counting" />`

- `value: number` The number it counts up to.
- `label: string` What the number counts, under it.
- `prefix?: string` Text before the number, such as `$`.
- `suffix?: string` Text after the number, such as `%` or `k`.
- `decimals?: number` Decimal places to show; 0 if not set.
- `note?: string` A small line under the label, such as a source or a date.

Needs: `text/counter`, `text/kinetic`

## Features · `templates/features`

Three to six features as cards with icons, coming in one after another: what the product does.

`<Features title="Everything in" accent="one window." items={[{icon: Columns3, title: 'Split panes', text: 'Grouped by project'}]} />`

- `eyebrow?: string` A small label above the headline.
- `title?: string` The headline, before its accent phrase.
- `accent?: string` The phrase set in the style's accent way, after the title.
- `items: { icon: LucideIcon; title: string; text?: string; }[]` Three to six features, one card each.

Needs: `text/kinetic`

## Quote · `templates/quote`

A testimonial with who said it, an initials avatar and their role; real quotes only, with permission.

`<Quote quote="I stopped tabbing through terminals." accent="Now they come to me." name="Ada Okafor" role="Design lead" />`

- `quote: string` What they said, before its accent phrase.
- `accent?: string` The phrase set in the style's accent way, after the quote.
- `name: string` Who said it; their initials make the avatar.
- `role?: string` Their role and company, under the name.

Needs: `text/kinetic`

## Compare · `templates/compare`

Before and after: the old way struck through, the product's way ticked, side by side or stacked.

`<Compare title="Stop checking" accent="every tab." before={{title: 'Before', items: ['Tab through every terminal']}} after={{title: 'After', items: ['Every agent in one view']}} />`

- `title?: string` The headline, before its accent phrase.
- `accent?: string` The phrase set in the style's accent way, after the title.
- `before: { title: string; items: string[]; }` The old way: a heading and its items, struck through.
- `after: { title: string; items: string[]; }` The product's way: a heading and its items, ticked.

Needs: `text/kinetic`

Numbered steps joined by a line that draws from one to the next: how it works.

`<Steps title="How it" accent="works." steps={['Open a group', 'Start your agents', 'Get pinged when one asks']} />`

- `title?: string` The headline, before its accent phrase.
- `accent?: string` The phrase set in the style's accent way, after the title.
- `steps: string[]` The steps in order, one short line each.

Needs: `text/kinetic`

## LogoReveal · `templates/logo-reveal`

The logo arriving (pop, iris, slide or focus), then the name and a tagline under it.

`<LogoReveal kind="pop" tagline="Run agents in parallel." accent="All in one place." />`

- `kind?: 'pop' | 'slide' | 'iris' | 'focus'` How the logo arrives: `pop`, `iris`, `slide` or `focus`; `pop` if not set.
- `tagline?: string` A line under the name, before its accent phrase.
- `accent?: string` The phrase set in the style's accent way, after the tagline.
- `logo?: string` An image in public/; the brand's logo if not set.
- `name?: string` The name under the logo; the brand's name if not set.

Needs: `text/kinetic`

## EndCard · `templates/end-card`

The ask, big and held: logo and name, one line, a button with a glint and the URL.

`<EndCard line="Your agents keep working." accent="Even when you walk away." button="Download free" url="example.com" />`

- `line?: string` One line under the name, before its accent phrase.
- `accent?: string` The phrase set in the style's accent way, after the line.
- `button?: string` The button's words; `Get started` if not set.
- `url?: string` Where to go, under the button; the brand's URL if not set.

Needs: `text/kinetic`

## Lineup · `templates/lineup`

People as portrait cards with initials or photos, coming in one after another: speakers, a team, hosts.

`<Lineup title="Meet the" accent="speakers." people={[{name: 'Ada Okafor', role: 'Design lead'}, {name: 'Ravi Menon', role: 'Founder'}]} />`

- `eyebrow?: string` A small label above the headline.
- `title?: string` The headline, before its accent phrase.
- `accent?: string` The phrase set in the style's accent way, after the title.
- `people: { name: string; role?: string; initials?: string; image?: string; }[]` Two to eight people, one card each.

Needs: `text/kinetic`

## Agenda · `templates/agenda`

A schedule row by row with times, then a highlight slides to the featured row: an event or a launch day.

`<Agenda title="Day one" date="Thu · Oct 24" items={[{time: '09:30', title: 'Keynote', who: 'Ravi Menon'}]} highlight={0} />`

- `eyebrow?: string` A small label above the headline.
- `title?: string` The headline, before its accent phrase.
- `accent?: string` The phrase set in the style's accent way, after the title.
- `date?: string` The day it happens, shown with the headline.
- `items: { time: string; title: string; who?: string; }[]` The schedule, one row each.
- `highlight?: number` The row to highlight, counted from 0; none if not set.

Needs: `text/kinetic`

## Countdown · `templates/countdown`

Days, hours, minutes and seconds ticking down for real, each digit rolling: a launch, an event, a sale ending.

`<Countdown title="Doors open" accent="soon." to="Oct 24 · 9:00 AM" from={{days: 12, hours: 4, minutes: 32, seconds: 9}} />`

- `eyebrow?: string` A small label above the headline.
- `title?: string` The headline, before its accent phrase.
- `accent?: string` The phrase set in the style's accent way, after the title.
- `to: string` The date and time it counts down to, as it should read.
- `from: { days: number; hours: number; minutes: number; seconds: number; }` How long is left when the scene starts; it ticks down in real time.

Needs: `text/kinetic`

## Changelog · `templates/changelog`

A version badge, then the changes landing one by one tagged New, Improved or Fixed: release notes.

`<Changelog version="v2.4" title="What's" accent="new." items={[{tag: 'New', text: 'Offline mode'}, {tag: 'Fixed', text: 'Sync on flaky connections'}]} />`

- `version: string` The release, in the badge, such as `v2.4`.
- `title?: string` The headline, before its accent phrase.
- `accent?: string` The phrase set in the style's accent way, after the title.
- `items: { tag: 'New' | 'Improved' | 'Fixed'; text: string; }[]` The changes, one row each.

Needs: `text/kinetic`

Big news: the headline lands with a glint and a drawn stroke under it, then a line of detail.

- `eyebrow?: string` A small label above the headline.
- `text: string` The news, before its accent phrase.
- `accent?: string` The phrase set in the style's accent way, after the text.
- `detail?: string` A line of detail under it.
- `size?: number` The headline's font size in px; sized to the frame if not set.

Needs: `text/kinetic`
