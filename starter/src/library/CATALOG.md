# The dolly library

GENERATED by `npm run library` from the items' folders: don't edit it. 87 items.

Every item is a folder in `src/library/<category>/<name>/`: the component in `<name>.tsx` and its
`item.json`. Import any of them from the library: `import {Stat, Kinetic, Statement} from './library'`
(`'../library'` from `src/scenes/`). Each reads the style through `useLook()` and lays itself out for
every format. `npm run gallery` renders every item's sample, labelled with its code.

## Templates

Whole scenes that take words as props. Put one in SCENES: `{name, seconds, component: () => <Statement text="…" />}`. They draw their own backdrop and lay themselves out for every format.

### Statement · `templates/statement`

One big line filling the frame, with an optional eyebrow: a hook, a punchline or a turn in the story.

`<Statement eyebrow="Agents everywhere" text="Five agents running." accent="One needs you." />`

Props: `eyebrow?: string` · `text: string` · `accent?: string` · `effect?: TextEffect` · `exit?: number`

Needs: `text/kinetic`

## Text

## Transitions

How one scene gives way to the next. A style picks one; a scene picks its own with `transition: '…'` (the one INTO that scene).

### cut · `transitions/cut`

A hard cut: the next scene replaces this one with no overlap.

`{name: 'Logo', seconds: 3, component: Logo, transition: 'cut'}`

### fade · `transitions/fade`

A cross-fade between the two scenes.

`{name: 'Logo', seconds: 3, component: Logo, transition: 'fade'}`

### dip · `transitions/dip`

The scene fades to the page colour, then the next one fades in from it.

`{name: 'Logo', seconds: 3, component: Logo, transition: 'dip'}`

### slide · `transitions/slide`

The next scene slides in from the right on a spring, pushing this one out.

`{name: 'Logo', seconds: 3, component: Logo, transition: 'slide'}`

### wipe · `transitions/wipe`

A hard edge sweeps across from the right, revealing the next scene.

`{name: 'Logo', seconds: 3, component: Logo, transition: 'wipe'}`

### iris · `transitions/iris`

A circle opens from the middle onto the next scene.

`{name: 'Logo', seconds: 3, component: Logo, transition: 'iris'}`

### clock · `transitions/clock`

A hand sweeps round like a clock, revealing the next scene.

`{name: 'Logo', seconds: 3, component: Logo, transition: 'clock'}`

### flip · `transitions/flip`

The frame turns over like a card in 3D to show the next scene.

`{name: 'Logo', seconds: 3, component: Logo, transition: 'flip'}`

### zoom · `transitions/zoom`

Forward through the cut: this scene rushes past the camera and blurs, the next settles in.

`{name: 'Logo', seconds: 3, component: Logo, transition: 'zoom'}`
