# The dolly library

GENERATED by `npm run library` from the items' folders: don't edit it. 87 items.

Every item is a folder in `src/library/<category>/<name>/`: the component in `<name>.tsx`, its
gallery sample in `<name>.demo.tsx` and its `item.json`. Import any of them from the library:
`import {Stat, Kinetic, Statement} from './library'` (`'../library'` from `src/scenes/`). Each
reads the style through `useLook()`, takes `start` (the frame it begins) and lays itself out for
every format.

This is the index. **Open a category's `CATALOG.md` for its items' usage lines and props**, and its
`preview.jpg` to see them (each cell is an item's sample, labelled with its code).

## Templates · [templates/CATALOG.md](templates/CATALOG.md) · [preview](templates/preview.jpg)

Whole scenes that take words as props. Put one in SCENES: `{name, seconds, component: () => <Statement text="…" />}`. They draw their own backdrop and lay themselves out for every format.

- **Statement**: One big line filling the frame, with an optional eyebrow: a hook, a punchline or a turn in the story.
- **Metric**: A number that counts up with what it counts under it; real numbers only.
- **Features**: Three to six features as cards with icons, coming in one after another: what the product does.
- **Quote**: A testimonial with who said it, an initials avatar and their role; real quotes only, with permission.
- **Compare**: Before and after: the old way struck through, the product's way ticked, side by side or stacked.
- **Steps**: Numbered steps joined by a line that draws from one to the next: how it works.
- **LogoReveal**: The logo arriving (pop, iris, slide or focus), then the name and a tagline under it.
- **EndCard**: The ask, big and held: logo and name, one line, a button with a glint and the URL.
- **Lineup**: People as portrait cards with initials or photos, coming in one after another: speakers, a team, hosts.
- **Agenda**: A schedule row by row with times, then a highlight slides to the featured row: an event or a launch day.
- **Countdown**: Days, hours, minutes and seconds ticking down for real, each digit rolling: a launch, an event, a sale ending.
- **Changelog**: A version badge, then the changes landing one by one tagged New, Improved or Fixed: release notes.
- **Timeline**: Milestones on a line that draws from one to the next, the last one marked: company history or a year in review.
- **List**: Labels with dotted leaders to values and an optional detail line: a menu, open roles, prices.
- **Announcement**: Big news: the headline lands with a glint and a drawn stroke under it, then a line of detail.

## Text · [text/CATALOG.md](text/CATALOG.md) · [preview](text/preview.jpg)

Kinetic type: a headline that animates in with its accent phrase marked, words that mark themselves, a rotating word, a counter. The style picks the effect and the mark; props override them.

- **Kinetic**: A headline that comes in word by word or letter by letter the style's way, with its accent phrase marked as it lands.
- **Mark**: Draws a highlighter, block, pill, underline, circle, strike or box on any inline words at a given frame.
- **Cycle**: One slot in a line whose word changes every few frames, sliding the old word out and the new one in.
- **Counter**: A number counting up or down with easing, thousands separators and tabular figures, at a fixed width.

## Graphics · [graphics/CATALOG.md](graphics/CATALOG.md) · [preview](graphics/preview.jpg)

Charts, numbers, pointers, input and product UI to put over any scene. Each takes `start` (the frame it begins) and most take `exit`.

- **Draw**: An SVG path that draws itself on, and off again: a hand-drawn underline, a scribble, a route.
- **Arrow**: A curved, hand-drawn arrow that draws itself from one point to another, its head landing last.
- **Ring**: A ring that fills to a percentage, the number counting up in its middle.
- **Bars**: A bar chart whose bars grow in one after another, values counting up, one bar highlighted.
- **LineChart**: A line chart that draws across, its area filling in and a dot riding the head with the current value.
- **Stat**: A big number counting up, its label under it, with an optional delta chip.
- **Progress**: A progress bar filling to a percentage, with its label and percentage above it.
- **Checklist**: Items ticking off one by one: each box fills and its check draws in.
- **Burst**: Confetti bursting from a point and falling away under gravity: a success moment.
- **Shine**: A glint of light sweeping across its children, once or on a loop.
- **Cursor**: A macOS pointer gliding through points, with a press and a ripple on clicks.
- **Keys**: Keyboard keys that pop in and press down one after another: a shortcut.
- **Spotlight**: Dims the whole frame except one rectangle, the hole tightening onto it.
- **Toast**: A notification sliding in like macOS or iOS: app icon, name, time, title and body.
- **Chat**: A conversation: bubbles arriving one by one, theirs after typing dots, earlier ones pushed up.
- **Badge**: A pill label that pops in with an icon or a live dot: New, Beta, Live.
- **Icon**: A lucide icon in a tinted tile that pops in, for feature grids.
- **Marquee**: A row of words or logos sliding past for ever, its ends fading out.

## Drawings · [drawings/CATALOG.md](drawings/CATALOG.md) · [preview](drawings/preview.jpg)

Illustrations drawn with code that animate as they draw. They tint themselves toward the brand colour.

- **Paper**: A card of warm paper with grain, fibres and an uneven edge that slides in and holds a drawing.
- **Flower**: A watercolour flower whose stem draws up, leaves unfurl and petals open from a bud.
- **Stamps**: A field of stamped motifs landing one after another in speckled ink.
- **HexField**: A board of hexagons warming from pale to deep around a hot spot, spreading in a wave.
- **Butterfly**: A butterfly that flutters in along a curve and hovers with beating wings.
- **Plant**: A plant growing from the bottom of its box: the stem draws up, branches fork, leaves unfurl and tips bloom.
- **Stitch**: Embroidery: a sun, circle, heart or leaf filled with satin stitches that appear as if sewn.
- **Watercolor**: A watercolour wash painted behind content, as a brushstroke or a spreading pool.
- **Doodles**: A hand-drawn mark in a wobbly pen line: underline, circle, arrow, star, heart and more.
- **Constellation**: Stars appearing one by one and joining up with fine lines into a constellation.
- **Contours**: Topographic contour lines of a made-up landscape, drawing on level by level.
- **Ripple**: Drops of ink landing one after another and rippling out in wobbly rings.
- **Scribble**: A marker scribbling back and forth to fill a shape behind a word or number.

## Voice · [voice/CATALOG.md](voice/CATALOG.md) · [preview](voice/preview.jpg)

A voiceover from WAV lines (`npm run voice`), captions in sync with it, and a waveform that moves with it.

- **Voiceover**: Plays a voiceover's lines one after another, and times scenes to them with voiceTiming and voiceEnd.
- **Captions**: Reels-style captions a few words at a time, timed to a voiceover, with the spoken word marked.
- **Waveform**: A waveform that moves with a voice: centred bars, a line, or bars around a ring with a face inside.

## Backdrops · [backdrops/CATALOG.md](backdrops/CATALOG.md) · [preview](backdrops/preview.jpg)

The page behind the shots. A style picks one; `<Backdrop kind="…" />` picks another.

- **plain**: Just the style's page colour.
- **glow**: Soft lights in the accent drifting over faint hairlines.
- **grid**: A swiss column grid inside the safe margins, with a + at every other crossing.
- **dots**: A dot grid that drifts slowly and fades at the edges.
- **blobs**: Pastel cousins of the accent, drifting and changing shape.
- **scanlines**: A CRT: scanlines, a rolling band and dark corners.
- **grain**: Film grain that dances every frame, with a vignette.
- **aurora**: Ribbons of light in the accent swaying over a hairline grid.
- **beams**: Light falling from above in slow-swaying beams.
- **spotlight**: One soft breathing light from above, the corners in shadow.

## Transitions · [transitions/CATALOG.md](transitions/CATALOG.md) · [preview](transitions/preview.jpg)

How one scene gives way to the next. A style picks one; a scene picks its own with `transition: '…'` (the one INTO that scene).

- **cut**: A hard cut: the next scene replaces this one with no overlap.
- **fade**: A cross-fade between the two scenes.
- **dip**: The scene fades to the page colour, then the next one fades in from it.
- **slide**: The next scene slides in from the right on a spring, pushing this one out.
- **wipe**: A hard edge sweeps across from the right, revealing the next scene.
- **iris**: A circle opens from the middle onto the next scene.
- **clock**: A hand sweeps round like a clock, revealing the next scene.
- **flip**: The frame turns over like a card in 3D to show the next scene.
- **zoom**: Forward through the cut: this scene rushes past the camera and blurs, the next settles in.
- **whip**: A very fast push sideways with a motion smear at its peak.
- **bars**: Bars of the accent sweep in, cover the scene, then uncover the next one.
- **flash**: A cut hidden in a bright flash, with a little punch-in on the next scene.
- **glitch**: A few frames of digital breakup: torn slices and split colour channels.
- **zoomBlur**: A radial zoom blur through the cut. A WebGL shader: use it as an accent, and check every frame of the final render around it.
- **crossZoom**: Both scenes zoom and blur into each other. A WebGL shader: use it as an accent, and check every frame of the final render around it.
- **dreamyZoom**: A slow, rotating dreamy zoom into the next scene. A WebGL shader: use it as an accent, and check every frame of the final render around it.
- **filmBurn**: A warm film burn flares across the cut. A WebGL shader: use it as an accent, and check every frame of the final render around it.
- **crosswarp**: The scenes warp sideways into each other. A WebGL shader: use it as an accent, and check every frame of the final render around it.
- **ripple**: A water ripple spreads from the middle into the next scene. A WebGL shader: use it as an accent, and check every frame of the final render around it.
- **swap**: The two scenes swap places in perspective. A WebGL shader: use it as an accent, and check every frame of the final render around it.
- **bookFlip**: A page turns like a book onto the next scene. A WebGL shader: use it as an accent, and check every frame of the final render around it.
- **blurSlide**: A slide with a motion blur. A WebGL shader: use it as an accent, and check every frame of the final render around it.
- **linearBlur**: A directional blur carries one scene into the next. A WebGL shader: use it as an accent, and check every frame of the final render around it.
- **pushCut**: A push cut with a flash in the accent at the join.
