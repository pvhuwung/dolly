# Voice

GENERATED by `npm run library`: don't edit it. A voiceover from WAV lines (`npm run voice`), captions in sync with it, and a waveform that moves with it.

`preview.jpg` shows every one: each cell is an item's sample, labelled with its code.

## Voiceover · `voice/voiceover`

Plays a voiceover's lines one after another, and times scenes to them with voiceTiming and voiceEnd.

`<Voiceover lines={tutorial} start={15} />`

- `lines: VoiceLine[]` The voiceover's lines, from src/voice/<name>.ts (written by npm run voice).
- `start?: number` Frame it starts on, counted from the start of its scene (default 0).
- `gap?: number` Seconds of silence between lines (default 0.3).
- `volume?: number` Its volume, 0 to 1 (default 1).

Also exports: `voiceTiming`, `voiceEnd`, `voiceWords`, `VoiceLine`, `Word`

## Captions · `voice/captions`

Reels-style captions a few words at a time, timed to a voiceover, with the spoken word marked.

`<Captions lines={tutorial} start={15} variant="karaoke" />`

- `lines?: VoiceLine[]` A voiceover's lines to caption; give it the Voiceover's start and gap.
- `only?: number[]` Not used by Captions, which shows every line; Waveform takes the same prop.
- `text?: string` Words to caption without a voiceover, spoken over `seconds` from `start`.
- `seconds?: number` Seconds `text` is spoken over (default 3).
- `words?: Word[]` Exact words with their start and end frames, instead of lines or text.
- `start?: number` Frame it starts on, counted from the start of its scene (default 0).
- `gap?: number` Seconds between the voiceover's lines, as given to Voiceover (default 0.3).
- `variant?: 'pop' | 'karaoke' | 'line'` karaoke (a box jumps to the word being said), pop (words pop in on their beat) or line (a plain subtitle); default karaoke.
- `max?: number` The most words on screen at once (default 3 in tall frames, 5 in wide ones).
- `position?: 'bottom' | 'middle'` Low in the safe area (bottom, the default) or in the middle.
- `size?: number` Its font size in px; sized to the frame if not set.
- `plate?: boolean` A plate behind the words: the default on light styles.

Needs: `voice/voiceover`

## Waveform · `voice/waveform`

A waveform that moves with a voice: centred bars, a line, or bars around a ring with a face inside.

`<Waveform lines={tutorial} start={15} variant="circle">{face}</Waveform>`

- `lines?: VoiceLine[]` A voiceover's lines to follow; give it the Voiceover's start and gap.
- `only?: number[]` The indices of the lines this waveform follows; the others leave it flat. Default: all.
- `gap?: number` Seconds between the voiceover's lines, as given to Voiceover (default 0.3).
- `src?: string` One audio file in public/ to follow, playing from `start`.
- `start?: number` Frame it starts on, counted from the start of its scene (default 0).
- `variant?: 'circle' | 'line' | 'bars'` bars (a centred spectrum), line (the wave) or circle (bars round a ring, with children inside); default bars.
- `width?: number` Its width in px, for bars and line (default 720).
- `height?: number` Its height in px, for bars and line (default 160).
- `size?: number` The circle's diameter in px (default 360).
- `bars?: number` How many bars (default 48, or 72 round a circle).
- `color?: string` Its colour; the style's accent if not set.
- `seed?: number` Seed for the made-up motion it shows with no voice to follow (default 3).
- `children?: ReactNode` What sits inside the circle: a face, a cover.

Needs: `voice/voiceover`
